Theme 1: Benefits of Vibrotactile feedback in accuracy of sound localisation and lowering cognitive load

- P1: "With the vibration, of course, because it gave me like a clear imaging"
- P3: "actually, I'm hearing the sounds better now in relation to audio alone"

benefits of a second modality:
- P1: "it's just adding another sensory approach to your workflow. I mean, it keeps you a little more engaged. So it was fun."
- P4: "And having another sense to assist that makes me feel a bit more comfortable with the sides (of the vest), or like with imagining the sound in the space.[...] once you have another sense to support your experience, at least I find it a bit more reassuring"

Reduction of Cognitive Load:
- P4: "It was very difficult with the audio, but with the vibrations, again, it felt like I could rely on another sense to give some more reassurance on what I was doing. So I felt like with the vest, it wasn't as mentally demanding as if I just had audio to focus on."
- P2: ""I don't think it's (cognitive) demanding in the slightest because it's using a different thing. You're feeling the music, you're hearing the music. Music is vibrations anyway. I think you made the experience a lot more enjoyable than it would have been, just hearing this screen reader. Often, I'll just turn the screen reader down so I can kind of hear what's going on, but it's not getting in the way of the music. But with this, you don't need to do that." 
- P1: "I did have the screen reader on. Whatever angle it was announcing, I didn't understand anything of that."

Resemblance to visual feedback:
- P3: "I'll say it's similar. For instance, in Logic, I know that it's in front; I'm seeing it. Here, I'm feeling it as well. Like from the vibration. So I'll say it's really similar."
- P1: "I think it just took the load off me. Sometimes when I'm working on my projects, I just feel too tired wearing the headphones and especially mixing because you just, you know, start noticing differences or, you know, you fail to come to a decision when you're mixing, because sometimes you end up being very confused with pannings, et cetera. So, with this vest, I can just put my headphones aside. I just sit there, but with this vest, I'm giving a break to one sensory approach, but the other is filling in, and my workflow doesn't stop, so maybe we'll lead to a faster workflow."
